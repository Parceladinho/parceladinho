Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Introduction

Lil Treat ("we," "us," or "the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app, which reads your credit card statements and shows you where your money goes.

2. Information we collect

Lil Treat collects only what it needs to work:

  • Your first name: optional, entered during setup and stored only on your device.
  • Statement data: the transactions, totals, and due dates read from your statements, plus the cards and savings goals you add. Stored on your device.
  • Statement files: the images or PDFs you choose to upload for AI reading. They're processed and not stored permanently on our servers.

3. How we use your information

We use your information only to:

  • Show your statements, transactions, and spending categories
  • Read the statements you upload
  • Send the reminders you turn on
  • Measure how well our ads work (see section 5)

4. On-device storage

Most of your information is stored on your device (and in your device backups, if you use them). We don't keep copies of your financial data on our servers. If you delete the app, everything it stored on your device is removed.

5. Data sharing and AI

We don't sell or rent your personal data.

When you upload a statement, the image or PDF is sent through our server to an artificial intelligence service (a large language model) that reads it. We send the statement file and the file's name and type. It's used only to extract the statement's transactions, totals, and dates and to suggest a category for each transaction. Statements aren't stored permanently after they're read. The AI service provides the same level of data protection described in this policy.

The app asks for your explicit consent before sending anything for AI reading.

Ad measurement. To measure our ads, the app uses Meta's software development kit to tell Meta about a few events: finishing setup, viewing the membership offer, uploading a statement, starting a free trial, and subscribing. Our subscription provider, RevenueCat, also reports purchases to Meta. These events never include your statements or transactions. On iPhone, Meta can link them to your activity in other companies' apps only if you allow tracking when the app asks. You can change this anytime in your iPhone's Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

6. Subscriptions

Subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't have access to your payment details. We use RevenueCat to check your subscription status. To manage your subscription, go to your Apple Account settings.

7. Security

We use technical measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for all data sent between the app and our servers.

8. Your choices

At any time, you can:

  • Delete your statements, cards, savings goals, and category corrections in the app (Settings › Delete my data)
  • Email us to ask about your information
  • Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device

9. Children

Lil Treat isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll let you know about significant changes in the app.
