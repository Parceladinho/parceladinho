Frequently asked questions

How do I upload a statement?

Tap the + button and choose a PDF, a photo, or a screenshot of your credit card statement. You can upload several files at once. For screenshots, make sure each line of the transaction list is fully in the frame — a line cut off at the edge can be misread.

What does the AI actually do?

It reads every line on your statement and sorts it into purchases, subscriptions, fees and interest, and payments and credits, with a suggested category for each charge. It doesn't connect to your bank and it can't move money. If it gets a category wrong, tap the charge to fix it — Lil Treat remembers your correction for future statements.

What leaves my phone?

Only the statement file you upload, plus its file name and type. It goes to our server, which passes it to our AI provider to read, and the file is deleted once it's been read. Everything the app shows you — your statements, cards, and savings goals — is stored on your phone, not on our servers. There's no bank login, so we never see your bank password. Details are in our Privacy Policy.

Which cards and banks work?

Any credit card that gives you a statement — Lil Treat doesn't connect to banks, so there's no list to be on. If your bank's PDF is password-protected, open the statement in your bank's app and upload screenshots instead.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Lil Treat is billed through your Apple Account, so you cancel the same way as any App Store subscription: on your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, tap Subscriptions, then Lil Treat, and choose Cancel. You'll keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for. Apple handles billing and refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I got a new phone. How do I get my subscription back?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you used to subscribe. Your statements live on your phone, so they come along only if you restored the new phone from a backup.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings and tap Delete my data. That removes your statements, cards, savings goals, and category corrections from your phone. Deleting the app does the same. We don't keep your financial data on our servers, so there's nothing else to delete — but email us if you'd like that in writing.
